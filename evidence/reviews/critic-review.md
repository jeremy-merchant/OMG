## Adversarial checks with no additional blocker

- **Authority hierarchy:** Product Spec is expressly the stable authority in the matrix and plan; public artifacts cite it rather than the retained private master provenance. No supporting artifact purports to replace it.
- **Acceptance, non-goal, and capability coverage:** Matrix rows cover the 21 standalone, 8 Pygmalion, and 8 Zoomzi checkboxes; NFR, gate, prohibition, non-goal, domain-capability, security, and privacy ledgers have stable IDs, procedures, evidence routes, phases, and cleanup. The plan’s primary-owner ledger assigns these ranges without treating cross-phase references as ownership transfer. C1 is the remaining uncovered product requirement found in this comparison.
- **Phase DAG and gates:** The plan orders D1 → P2-A/B → P3/P4 → P5 → retain/rename decision → D6 RC gate → D7, with D8 additionally awaiting Pygmalion evidence and its own approval. This is stricter than, and compatible with, the Product Spec requirement that the standalone RC gate pass before either proof begins. D6 explicitly gates all capability, security, and privacy IDs before proofs.
- **Migration and restore authority:** Matrix `OMG-AC-016`, `SEC-T14`–`015`, `PRIV-T05`, and Gate interpretation require non-mutating discovery/plan behavior and per-apply approval binding. The plan binds each migration apply to a specific plan, fixture/project, schema range, checksums, verified backup, command, and UTC timestamp, and treats restore apply as separately human-approved. C1 restores only to a newly validated disposable destination and verifies inventory/checksums; it does not overlay a fixture.
- **Identity and RC invalidation:** Product Spec working-identity status, matrix `OMG-AC-019`/`OMG-RG-002`, and plan §§1/4 consistently require the private retain/rename decision before the final local RC pin. A later identity change invalidates the RC and requires rebuilding plus rerunning both proof projects and parity evidence; none treats that decision as publication or legal clearance.
- **Proof-harness isolation and Pygmalion idempotency:** The plan locates both proof harnesses outside core, limits them to released CLI/OMGCP interfaces and generic opaque records, and prohibits proof-specific core imports, fixtures, build tags, and conditional branches. Matrix `OMG-PYG-004` requires two real applications of the same approved pinned import in a disposable clone, comparing normalized canonical state, receipt/idempotency sets, append-only history, and historical-file hashes; a dry run is not substituted for either apply.
- **Cross-platform evidence:** Matrix uses a Windows-portable UTC directory component and stores the full RFC3339 instant in metadata. It explicitly identifies CI-only cross-platform claims and requires an evidence subtree from every required runner; the plan rejects a local result as a substitute.
- **Destructive-operation and rollback boundaries:** C0–C4 and plan rules confine cleanup to fixture/archive, marker-only, approved disposable-clone, or report archival procedures. They prohibit destructive Git commands and real-project mutation. `SAFE_TO_REMOVE`, handoffs, messages, tokens, and `VERIFIED_DONE` remain non-authorizing.
- **Security/privacy D6 gate:** Matrix Gate interpretation requires all `OMG-CAP-*`, `SEC-T*`, `SEC-E*`, `PRIV-T*`, and `PRIV-E*` evidence and required dispositions before D6 passes; plan D6 owns the full ranges. This review does not treat future evidence or human dispositions as complete.
- **Architecture blockers:** The current Architect re-review resolves A1 by making `internal/app/query` the immutable, authorized, redacted snapshot owner and `internal/view` a pure consumer; it resolves A2 through exclusive `0003_reservations.sql` and `0004_git_assets.sql` ownership with P2-B-only ordered application. Current plan anchors are mutually consistent. The initial Architect `REVISE` remains immutable historical evidence.
